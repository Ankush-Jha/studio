# Reference board, 2026-11-16

56 references for the stills library (`.claude/skills/stills/`, `engines/stills/`) and the motion templates (`engines/motion/`).

**How it was gathered.** WebSearch on Pinterest, Behance, Dribbble, Figma, Instagram and trend sources; single logged-out page views with robots.txt respected (a polite one-page screenshot script). Pinterest and Instagram robots.txt disallow our bot and Behance returns 403, so those are search-sourced only. Screenshots were private analysis copies and are not included; nothing is to be reused. Hex values marked 'sampled' were read from our screenshot and are approximate.

Style slugs: swiss, terminal, bento, magazine, annotated, cutout, riso, bold-type, isometric, paper-trail, broadsheet, mapcard. Motion templates: pop-hook, phone-pov, object-3d, kinetic-hook.

## Design brief: 24 testable rules

I derived these from the board above and checked them against our own renders (`engines/stills/out/library-overview.jpg`, the motion strips). What our renders lack most, compared with the references:
- **Covers carry too many words.** Many slide 1s carry 12–20 words; the references use one object and 6 words or fewer.
- **Hero numbers are 45–60% of the width.** The references fill the width, or make the number the object.
- **Objects are rare and small.**
- **Small furniture (kicker, label row, compliance card) competes on every slide.**
- **Navy/blue is used in four styles.** That is the category default (see the Dribbble anti-board).
- **Nothing crosses slide edges.**

| # | Rule | How to test | Source refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero number fills ≥ 55% of the canvas width** on any number-led slide (≥ 594 px of 1080), and nothing else on the slide is more than ⅓ of its cap height. For a cover/hook number, aim for ≥ 70% of the safe width. | bbox of the hero element ÷ 1080 | ws-3numbers, ramp-brand, bbw-covers |
| 2 | **Slide 1 = one object + 6 words or fewer** (headline). Kicker ≤ 4 words, and no sub-line on covers. The object (code-drawn, a client photo, or the number as object) covers ≥ 30% of the area. | word count; object bbox area | ws-realtor, ws-3numbers, joeyemond |
| 3 | **Maximum 2 typefaces per post** (a mono for data counts as one). **Maximum 3 sizes per slide** (hero / text / label), with each step ≥ 1.6× the next. | computed styles in the rendered HTML | visualize-value, semafor, mwk-social |
| 4 | **One accent per slide, ≤ 15% of pixels**, used only on the thing the slide is about (the series, the number, the struck word). | hue histogram | economist-ig, ramp-brand, mwk-social |
| 5 | **Maximum 4 colours per post** including ink and paper. | palette clustering over all slides | ws-3numbers (3 colours), monzo-coral |
| 6 | **Flat committed fields.** ≥ 60% of each slide is one solid field or paper tone. No gradient with > 2 stops, no glow, no glass, no drop shadows except physically motivated ones (cut paper, objects on a floor). | pixel share of the modal colour; CSS lint | ws-*, ft-paper, canva-imperfect |
| 7 | **Not navy-and-white by default.** A client may keep a brand blue, but the field system has at least one non-blue field (paper, coral, green, yellow, lilac) and blue is never the field on slide 1 *and* the last slide. | field colour per slide | monzo-coral, klarna-pink, dribbble-mortgage-anti |
| 8 | **Thumbnail test:** at 270 px wide (the profile grid), the slide-1 headline and hero are readable. In practice: slide-1 headline ≥ 88 px at 1080 width, hero ≥ 220 px. | downscale render; reviewer reads it in 2 s | apple-bento, visualize-value |
| 9 | **Headlines are conclusions with a number**, not topics. "Prices rose faster here than anywhere in the G7", not "Housing prices 2019–2026". | regex: a chart-slide title contains a verb and a digit | ws-4charts, economist-ig |
| 10 | **One chart, one message:** one accent series, everything else in a neutral at ≤ 40% contrast, direct labels instead of legends, and a source line on every chart slide. | chart spec lint (no legend, 1 highlighted series, `source` set) | economist-ig, viscap-housing, chartr |
| 11 | **Statement/evidence rhythm.** Carousels alternate a statement slide (field + big type) and an evidence slide (paper + chart, document or object). No 3 consecutive slides share a layout. | layout id sequence | ws-4charts |
| 12 | **Swipe trigger on slide 1:** an element crosses the right edge by ≥ 40 px, or there is an arrow/counter. And **an open loop on every other slide** (a question, a half-shown number, "but…"). | edge-crossing detection; copy lint | PLAYBOOK, carousel-trends-2026 |
| 13 | **At least one seamless join per carousel:** an object or line crosses x = 1080 between two adjacent slides with pixel-exact alignment. | render a 2-slide strip and diff the boundary columns | carousel-trends-2026, figma-easy-carousels |
| 14 | **Word budget:** cover headline ≤ 6, body slides ≤ 20, recap ≤ 40. The existing ≤ 40 cap stays for the recap only. | word count per slide | PLAYBOOK, virallized-30 (text-heavy is tired) |
| 15 | **Objects over icons.** The hero visual is a specific object rendered in code (a renewal letter, a stack of slips, a door, a house volume sized from the number, a receipt). Library icons are ≤ 48 px, as labels only. | reviewer, plus a neardup check against the icon sets | ws-realtor, receiptify, ws-floorplans |
| 16 | **Number as object** at least once per carousel: the figure is extruded, stacked, printed on a document, or sized geometry, not only flat type. | reviewer | ws-3numbers, viscap-housing (3D bars) |
| 17 | **One emphasis device per headline:** highlight, italic swap, strike-and-replace, or colour. Never two. | markup lint (`==`, `*`, `~~` count per headline ≤ 1) | mwk-social |
| 18 | **One material signal per style** (paper grain, halftone/overprint, phosphor scanline, matte clay, newsprint), seeded and deterministic, 2–6% opacity, never under live text below 28 px. | determinism test; contrast measured on real pixels (existing gate) | riso-windowseat, vox-collage, canva-imperfect |
| 19 | **Quiet furniture.** Kicker, label row, folio and compliance line together occupy ≤ 8% of the slide area and are never the highest-contrast element. The compliance line stays legible (≥ 20 px, 4.5:1) and present. | area and luminance of the furniture nodes | ws-4charts (tiny CHART n kicker) |
| 20 | **Grid discipline:** text boxes on a slide start from ≤ 3 distinct x positions, all on the style's declared grid. | left-edge x values from the DOM | swiss-modernism-pin, vignelli-subway |
| 21 | **One specific, local detail per post** (place, Canadian reference, community, language) that a template couldn't contain. | reviewer; ties to the Playbook sameness rule | ws-realtor ('Timbits') |
| 22 | **Faces are real and framed.** Client photos are only cut out, pill-cropped or used as a frame. Never stock couples. | reviewer | mwk-social, pc-jolicoeur, dribbble-mortgage-anti |
| 23 | **Tabular lining numerals everywhere.** The currency sign is set at 55–65% of numeral size, top-aligned, and the number format is identical across slides. | font-feature-settings `tnum`; format lint | economist-ig, ramp-brand |
| 24 | **Series recognisability:** per client, the slide-1 hero position and field logic are fixed across posts, so the grid reads as one account. | compare slide-1 layouts across the client's last 6 posts | visualize-value, monzo-coral |

## Carousel slide-to-slide patterns (swipe triggers)

1. **Statement → evidence → statement** (ws-4charts). Each statement slide makes a claim that the next slide proves.
2. **Same camera, one change** (ws-floorplans). Two slides with an identical object and composition; swiping *is* the comparison. Use it for fixed vs variable, before vs after renewal, 25 vs 30 years.
3. **One number per slide, same field** (ws-3numbers). Counting slides ("3 numbers and one day") gives a progress mechanism; the "and one day" breaks the pattern on the last slide.
4. **Quiz → answer** (ws-pricequiz). The question on slide N is answered on slide N+1; the answer chip is the only colour.
5. **Strike-and-replace** (mwk-social). Slide 1 shows the myth figure; slide 2 shows it struck through with the real figure beside it.
6. **Object crosses the seam** (carousel-trends-2026). A road, a chart line or a stack of slips runs from slide 1 into slide 2; half an object at the right edge forces the swipe.
7. **Document zoom** (receiptify, paper-trail). Slide 1 shows the whole letter or receipt; each next slide is a marked-up crop of one line.
8. **✓ / ✗ pair** (ws-realtor). "Want" slide then "don't want" slide in identical pill rows: savable, sendable.
9. **Mixed clip** (carousel-trends-2026). One 5–10 s motion slide after slide 3 resets attention, and lets the post surface in Reels with a Sound Collection track.

## Trends for late 2026

**Rising**
- Hand-made marks on precise data: marker circles, tape, stickers, strike-throughs (canva-imperfect, carousel-trends-2026).
- Editorial and newspaper layouts; documents and receipts as formats.
- Expressive serifs with italic swaps; local vernacular type (figureestudio).
- Collage with colour only on the reveal (Spotify Wrapped 2025).
- Number-as-object and matte, physical 3D used once per post, not everywhere.
- Seamless carousels; mixed video-in-carousel; quiz slides.
- 1080×1440 (3:4) carousels.

**Tired**
- Aurora/mesh and grainy gradients.
- Glassmorphism cards outside Apple's own UI.
- Glossy 3D blobs and "gratuitous 3D".
- Bento as a SaaS reflex (use it only for 6–9 parallel facts).
- Neon on black; Swiss-template Helvetica with no data.
- "5 tips" text walls; centred hero with three icons.
- Navy-and-gold "luxury" broker templates; house-roof logos; stock couples.
- Puffy/chrome lettering is cooling.
