# Studio Playbook: Instagram content for mortgage brokers

Last researched: 16 November 2026 (113 sources on platform rules and craft). Every reel, carousel, static and script made with Studio follows this file. It covers Instagram's current rules, the craft (hooks, scripts, directing, editing), and compliance. Platform rules change: re-check the specs and dates before relying on them.

## The five rules that matter most

1. **Frame 1 decides.** In the first second, the spoken hook, the on-screen text (3–7 words, two lines at most) and the visual say the same thing. The topic goes in the first line. No greeting, logo sting or intro card. Instagram measures the **skip rate** for the first 3 seconds.
2. **Every second earns its place.** Write the last line first. End right after the payoff, with no recap, outro card or "follow for more". The last beat loops into the first. Cutting one second from an ending has moved retention from 83% to 88%.
3. **Design for sends.** Instagram's top signals are watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach**. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers. Before scripting, name who would forward the post and to whom.
4. **Original, per account.** Since 30 Apr 2026, accounts that mostly post content they didn't create, or only lightly changed, lose recommendations, in every format. When one idea goes to several clients, give each client a different opening line, local angle, example or voice. Other apps' watermarks block recommendations entirely.
5. **Trust is part of the craft.** Keep a peer tone, and let the authority come from correct math. Show the brokerage name and licence number where the province requires it (Ontario: every ad). Never say "guaranteed", "best rate" or "lowest rate". Label numbers as illustrative and date any rate.

## Instagram specs (Nov 2026)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Reel canvas | 1080×1920, 9:16. Instagram plays up to 2K (1440×2560) at 60 fps. Minimum 30 fps in the app; the API accepts 23–60 fps. |
| Length | **3:00 or less**, or the reel only reaches followers. Best average views in 2026: **45–60 s**. Under 30 s only for one-number reels. |
| Text safe zone | Keep clear of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% on each side. On 1080×1920, **all text sits inside x 65–1015, y 270–1248**. |
| Cover | 1080×1920, with the headline inside the centre 3:4 band (**y 240–1680**), because the profile grid crops to 3:4. Set the cover before publishing. |
| Carousel | Up to 20 slides at 1080×1440 (3:4). Use 1080×1350 (4:5) if it may be boosted or scheduled through the API. |
| Caption | 2,200 characters. Line 1 is the plain-language search phrase plus the hook ("Mortgage renewal Ontario: …"). |
| Hashtags | **5 maximum**, counting tags in comments (since 18 Dec 2025). Specific ones only (topic + city). They don't drive reach. |
| Audio | Business accounts use original voice or Meta Sound Collection only. Never trending licensed songs: they can't be boosted. |
| AI | Label realistic AI voices or video with Instagram's AI label. **TTS voiceovers (e.g. Kokoro) count.** |
| Boosting | A mortgage post is a Special Ad Category (credit) ad: ages 18–65+, all genders, at least a 25 km radius, no lookalikes. |
| Upload | Turn on "Upload at highest quality" on every posting device. |

## Script

- **Words:** about 150 per minute.

  | Length | Words |
  |---|---|
  | 15 s | 35–40 |
  | 30 s | 70–80 |
  | 45–60 s | 110–150 |
  | 90 s | 200–225 |

- **Joining beats:** link them with **"but" or "therefore"**, never "and then". Foreshadow the payoff within 3 s, and re-hook every 5–8 s.
- **Write for the ear:** one idea per sentence, grade 5–7 words, read it aloud. Define jargon (IRD, LTV, GDS/TDS) the first time, or cut it.
- **Numbers:** at most one per sentence. Round it in the voice ("about two-eighty a month") and show the exact figure on screen with assumptions (balance, rate, amortization) marked "illustrative".
- **One promise per video.** Give the viewer a mechanism to follow, such as numbered steps, a countdown or a closing circle.
- **Hooks:** write three versions (curiosity, bold claim, story start) and test them as **trial reels**. Trial reels go to non-followers first.
- **Shapes:**
  - 15 s one-number punch
  - 30 s mistake → fix
  - 30–45 s myth → truth
  - 45–60 s problem–agitate–solve
  - 45–60 s client story (before–after–bridge)
  - 90 s walk-through
  - 10–20 s meme/POV
  - 8–10 slide carousel

### Hook patterns that work
- **Costly mistake:** "The renewal mistake that can cost you thousands: signing the first offer."
- **Myth snapback:** "Everyone says you need 20% down. In Canada, that's not the minimum."
- **Exact-number cold open:** "About $280 a month. That's roughly what each 1% of rate adds on $500K."
- **Same X, different outcome:** "Same buyer, same $500K mortgage. One pays about $67K more. One decision."
- **Callout + deadline:** "If your mortgage renews in the next six months, watch this before you sign."
- **One question decides:** "Fixed or variable? One question decides it, and it isn't today's rate."
- **Mark the date:** "Mark this date: four months before your renewal."
- **I ran the numbers:** "I changed one payment setting on a $500K mortgage. It cut about three years off."

### CTA (exactly one)
Choose one of:
- a **send** ("Send this to the friend renewing this year")
- a **save** ("Save this for the day your renewal letter arrives")
- a **comment keyword that delivers something** ("Comment RENEWAL for my 5-question checklist")

Never engagement bait ("Comment YES", "Tag 3 friends"). The CTA goes in the payoff line or the caption, not an outro card.

## Copy that reads as AI (banned)
Added after the 16 Nov audit.
- **Banned phrases:**
  - "That's not how it works"
  - "Nobody tells you"
  - "Here's the thing"
  - "Let's dive in"
  - "may surprise you"
  - "game-changer"
  - "unlock"
  - "dream home"
  - "The point?"
  - "Sound familiar?"
- **Banned patterns:**
  - "It's not X, it's Y" closers
  - aphorism endings
  - lists of three for rhythm
  - em-dash asides
- **Negation hooks** ("X is not a penalty", "not free money") only when the myth is genuinely widespread and you can attach a number.
- **Hook templates** describe how a hook works. Never copy their wording.
- **Sameness is the biggest AI tell.** Never send one post to many accounts with only the city swapped. Each client's version differs in topic angle, hook and visual device, and uses at least two facts from their profile: communities, languages, niche or location.
- **Math to get right:** a HELOC is usually interest-only, so +0.25% ≈ $21 a month per $100K. A 25-year amortizing mortgage ≈ $13–14 per $100K (Canadian semi-annual compounding). Show the working for every number.
- **Rate premises expire.** Date-stamp anything built on current rates, review it after 30 days, and re-render or retire it.

## Statics and carousels (production)
- Code-rendered templates (Studio Stills, `engines/stills/`): every word, number and licence line is live type, never generated by an image model.
- Real or licensed photos for any person. AI only for faceless backgrounds, textures and props, always graded to match the set.
- Eleven house styles in four groups, plus per-client brand tokens. Run a near-duplicate image check before export.
- 1080×1350 master, body text ≥ 28 px, ≤ about 40 words per slide, and WCAG AA contrast.
- No purple/teal gradients, glow, generic keys-and-handshake stock or waxy AI faces. If a reviewer's first reaction is "that looks AI", reject it.

## Directing graphic reels (Studio Films and Studio Motion productions)

- Design each beat's visual before finalizing the words. Every beat must make sense **on mute**.
- Use **one visual metaphor or a recurring character/journey** for the whole reel, e.g. a house filling with coins, or a buyer walking from pre-approval to keys.
- Frame 1 uses a visual hook: a text overlay, a pattern interrupt, subject motion or an effect. No logo bumper.
- Kinetic type:
  - **About 2 words per second** (3 at most). Only fully settled text counts as reading time.
  - Contrast of **4.5:1** or better, with a backing panel over busy frames.
  - Captions in **3–5-word chunks** with one highlighted keyword, broken at natural phrase points.
- Pacing: **something changes every 2–4 s**, nothing stays static past 4 s, and nothing flashes more than 3 times per second.
- Motion and brand:
  - One motion style per series, and only one element leads at a time.
  - Per broker: one typeface in two weights, 3–4 colours, the same caption position and the same way of revealing numbers.
- Sound:
  - Voice at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak under −1 dBTP.
  - Music 18–25 dB under the voice, with fast ducking.
  - A whoosh on transitions and a hit on number reveals (SFX −12 to −18 dB under the dialogue).
  - No dead silence mid-reel, and energy rises toward the payoff.
- **Loop:** the final frame matches the opening composition, motion carries through the last frame, and there's **no end card**.

## Directing face reels (brokers filming on a phone)

- **Camera:**
  - Rear camera, native 9:16, 4K30, AE/AF lock on the face.
  - Lens at eye level, chest-up framing, eyes on the top-third line.
- **Light:** a window or soft key light in front at 45°. Never a window behind, and overhead lights off.
- **Sound:** a lav mic 15–30 cm from the mouth, appliances off, and 10 s of room tone.
- **Opening:** start with motion or a prop already in hand (renewal letter, calculator), never "Hi, I'm…". Energy about 20% above normal conversation.
- **Delivery:**
  - Look into the lens; put the teleprompter at the lens.
  - Experienced brokers speak from bullet points.
  - After a fluff, pause 2 s and restart the sentence.
- **Edit:**
  - Cut pauses over 0.3 s.
  - Punch in to 110–120% every few cuts.
  - Cut to b-roll whenever the script names something visual.
- **Wardrobe:** solid mid-tones, no fine patterns, no logos.
- **Batch filming:** plan a 20-minute session that covers several scripts with one setup, outfit changes between them.

## Carousels

- **Slide 1** is a hook, not a title.
- **Slide 2** is a second hook, because Instagram often shows it first to people who didn't swipe.
- **Every slide:** one idea, about 20 words at most, in large type.
- **Length:** 8–10 slides.
- **The end:** the second-to-last slide is a save-worthy recap, and the last slide asks for one action.
- **Swipe cues:** add an arrow on slide 1 and elements that cross the slide edges.
- **Sound:** add a Sound Collection track, so the carousel can also surface in Reels.

## Compliance and trust

- **Ontario (O. Reg. 188/08 s.6):** every ad, Reels included, clearly shows the brokerage's authorized name and licence number. Agents also show their licensed name and approved title. Put this in the content or the first caption lines, not only the bio.
  - A generic end card without these is a proof, not a post: confirm the wording with each broker of record before posting in Ontario.
- **Advertised rate or payment (O. Reg. 191/08 s.18):** show the APR and term just as prominently, and say them if the rate is spoken.
- **Banned language:** "guaranteed approval", "best/lowest rate", fear-mongering, "secret loophole".
- **Comparisons:** a named-lender comparison is the highest-risk format, so label it general information.
- **AI:** label human-sounding AI voices. Never present an AI presenter as the broker.

## Delivery spec for finished files

- **Video:** H.264, yuv420p, BT.709 limited range, `+faststart`, at least 30 fps for new work (24 fps is accepted by the API).
- **Audio:** AAC 48 kHz. Use **128 kbps** when posting through the API or a scheduler.
- **Size and contents:** under 25 Mbps and 300 MB, with no third-party watermark.
- **Cover:** a separate 1080×1920 `poster.jpg` with the title in the centre 3:4 band.

## Pre-flight checklist

1. The one-sentence point and single promise are written down.
2. The topic is in the first spoken line, with no wind-up.
3. The hook is aligned: voice, visual and text say the same thing in frame 1.
4. Frame 1 works as a silent thumbnail, with no logo.
5. It passes the mute test.
6. Text is inside the safe zone (y 270–1248, x 65–1015).
7. The payoff is foreshadowed within 3 s, beats are joined with "but/therefore", and there's a re-hook every 5–8 s.
8. The word count fits the length at 150 wpm.
9. Numbers: one per sentence, rounded in the voice, exact and labelled on screen, rates dated.
10. Plain words throughout, with jargon defined.
11. The visual changes every 2–4 s and nothing flashes more than 3 times a second.
12. Captions are 3–5-word chunks with contrast of 4.5:1 or better.
13. Audio is at −14 LUFS, music sits 18–25 dB under the voice, and it comes from the Sound Collection or is original.
14. It's original for this client's account, with no other app's watermark.
15. It ends on the payoff, has no end card, and loops cleanly.
16. There's exactly one CTA, and it isn't bait.
17. Compliance line present where required; AI voice labelled.
18. The cover headline is inside the centre 3:4 band.
19. The send test passes: you can name who forwards it, and to whom.
20. Caption line 1 has the search phrase, and there are no more than 5 hashtags.

## Common faults in early reels

Found when the first reels were audited against this playbook. Check for them on every re-cut:
- **End cards:** ending on a generic "Message me" card. Cut it and loop instead.
- **Safe area:** text running down to about y 1480, below the y 1248 bottom limit.
- **AI label:** TTS voiceovers posted without Instagram's AI label.
- **Audio bitrate:** about 192 kbps AAC from editors. Re-encode to 128 kbps if posting through the API.
- **Frame rate:** 24 fps renders. New work should be 30 fps.
